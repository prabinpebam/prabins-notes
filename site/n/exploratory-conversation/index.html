Making complex products feel simple

Windows has required designing for a broad range of users, capabilities and system constraints. Rubrik faces a different problem, but the underlying design challenge—reducing cognitive load without sacrificing capability—is relevant.

Scaling design leadership

Your experience building teams, establishing design ownership and connecting quality with business impact could be relevant to a leader scaling a global design organization.

Rubrik is moving toward agentic experiences

Key development

In June 2026, Rubrik announced Rubrik AI, introducing agent-first experiences across Rubrik Security Cloud and Rubrik Agent Cloud. The stated ambition is to let organizations specify desired outcomes while AI reasons and acts across the platform.

Why this matters for design

  • From tools to outcomes: Users may specify “recover clean” or “contain the blast radius” rather than manually navigate dozens of security workflows.

  • Trust and control: How do you communicate what an agent plans to do, what it has done, what it cannot safely do, and when a human must intervene?

  • Complexity versus simplicity: Hiding technical details can make a product easier to use, but it can also hide important risks from security professionals.

  • Systems, not screens: AI agents need coherent interaction models, shared design patterns, consistent permissions and understandable failure states across many products.

These are design implications of Rubrik's announced direction, not claims about Mukul's individual projects.

Rubrik's fiscal

Rubrik's fiscal 2026 shareholder letter also emphasizes the convergence of data, identity and AI, and reports $1.46 billion in subscription annual recurring revenue, up 34% year over year. This suggests a business scaling into a broader platform, not simply adding an AI chat box to an existing product.
