# Democratized designing

My experience trying to design for workflows that leverages AI capabilities has been very weird to say the least.

The speed at which things change has been a challenge. Most of the product team including the Engineers are clueless about the new ai capabilities. Many time a more driven Designer knows so much more about the tech and what it can do even more than the Engineer.

A traditional product development process takes a few months to build conviction and to be validated for real with real adoption and usage. We were sitting trying to figure out user scenarios and the open source communities are coming up with throwaway projects at breakneck speed and the good ones are getting organic and natural adoption.

It felt too proud to sit and assume only our team can sit and figure out the right user scenarios where the real world already made a product and started using it daily when we were still brainstorming.

Claude code and OpenClaw are two such examples. Never meant for the masses but the coders they were meant for in the beginning cant's be prevented from using it like normal humans to do normal human things as they happen to be also humans. All of the mainstream use cases were organically defined and discovered by them.&#x20;
