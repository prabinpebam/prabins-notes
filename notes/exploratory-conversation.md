## Rubrik is moving toward agentic experiences

Key development

In June 2026, Rubrik announced Rubrik AI, introducing agent-first experiences across Rubrik Security Cloud and Rubrik Agent Cloud. The stated ambition is to let organizations specify desired outcomes while AI reasons and acts across the platform.

### Why this matters for design

- From tools to outcomes: Users may specify “recover clean” or “contain the blast radius” rather than manually navigate dozens of security workflows.
- Trust and control: How do you communicate what an agent plans to do, what it has done, what it cannot safely do, and when a human must intervene?
- Complexity versus simplicity: Hiding technical details can make a product easier to use, but it can also hide important risks from security professionals.
- Systems, not screens: AI agents need coherent interaction models, shared design patterns, consistent permissions and understandable failure states across many products.

These are design implications of Rubrik's announced direction, not claims about Mukul's individual projects.

## Rubrik's fiscal

Rubrik's fiscal 2026 shareholder letter also emphasizes the convergence of data, identity and AI, and reports $1.46 billion in subscription annual recurring revenue, up 34% year over year. This suggests a business scaling into a broader platform, not simply adding an AI chat box to an existing product.

# Questions

### Priority 1

“As AI agents start taking actions across enterprise products, what do you think becomes the core design problem? Is it still interaction design, or is it increasingly about trust, delegation and control?”\
Why ask: It connects directly to Rubrik's agent-first strategy and invites him to share his own perspective rather than repeat the product announcement.

### Priority 2

“How is AI changing the way your design team works today—not just what you ship, but the actual design-to-engineering workflow?”\
Why ask: This creates a natural opening to discuss your thinking on production-code workflows, agentic guardrails and the changing role of design tools.

### Priority 3

“When you're scaling a global design organization, how do you maintain a high bar for craft while giving teams the autonomy to move quickly?”\
Why ask: It explores a real leadership tension that is relevant to your experience managing designers and building organizational capability.

### Priority 4

“What do you look for in senior design leaders today that you wouldn't necessarily have looked for five years ago?”

### Follow up

- “What has been hardest to change inside the organization—skills, processes, or expectations from product and engineering?”

- “Where do you think experienced designers have an unfair advantage over people who are simply very good at using AI tools?”
